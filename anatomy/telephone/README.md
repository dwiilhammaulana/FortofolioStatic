# Telephone Anatomy Figure

## Concept

Satu telepon meja klasik mengisi ruang kosong pada panel pekerjaan saat ini. Handset menjadi subjek utama, sedangkan badan, rotary dial, cradle, dan kabel spiral menjelaskan perangkat secara visual tanpa label tambahan.

## Parts list

Figure ini terdiri dari 58 bagian visual:

- Base: top shell, front shell, left shell, rear ledge, front lip, label plate, two feet, four corner screws, cord port, cord connector.
- Rotary mechanism: outer bezel, dial ring, inner ring, centre hub, centre cap, ten finger holes, stop finger, dial marker, three mounting dots.
- Handset: outer body, inner grip, two cup bodies, two cup rings, six acoustic slots, two neck seams, two cradle posts, two cradle pads.
- Cord: lead-in, twelve spiral turns, tail, and terminal dot.

The visible result intentionally uses white strokes only. Dark shape fills match the surrounding charcoal panel solely to keep rear edges from showing through foreground parts.
